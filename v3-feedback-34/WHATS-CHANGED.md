## One deliberate difference from last round

On the banner, four deleted lines were treated as budget: the space went back
into the subject line and the phone number so they read from further away.

The card is the opposite call. It is read at arm's length, so size was never the
problem — noise was. The space freed here is left as space. Nothing grew into
it, and the line count on the back went from eight to seven while the card got
visibly emptier.

`business-card-before-and-after.png` puts both sides side by side at the same
size, trimmed. It is deliberately not blurred: blurring a banner is a fair test
of a banner, but blurring something you hold in your hand proves nothing.
