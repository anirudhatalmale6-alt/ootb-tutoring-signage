## What that bought

Four deletions take roughly nine inches of type off a four-foot banner. That
space is not left empty — the two things that do the work from the road grew
into it:

| | v1 | v2 | readable at |
|---|---|---|---|
| MATH · SCIENCE · ENGLISH | 2.60 in cap | 3.18 in cap | 95 ft |
| Phone number | 5.95 in cap | 6.14 in cap | 184 ft |
| Hook line | — | 0.95 in cap | 28 ft |

`banner-before-and-after.png` puts the two side by side and blurs both by the
same amount, which is the closest thing to a fair test of "does it read from the
road" without standing in the road.

## Two things to decide

1. **`banner-6ft-x-4ft-landscape-ALT-in-person-and-online.pdf`** — "Only have
   Grades 1-12" could mean *keep only Grades 1-12 on that line* or *change the
   wording to Grades 1-12*. The main file takes the first reading. The ALT file
   is the same banner with `IN PERSON AND ONLINE` still on it, in case that is
   what you meant. Pick one and the other gets deleted.

2. **The flyer, the business card and sign face B keep the course names.** The
   four deletions are about clutter at a distance, and those three pieces are
   read in the hand or once you have stopped — on sign face B the course list is
   the entire point of that face. Say the word and they come off there too.
